# Code of conduct

Kinora is made by people in their spare time, for people who love watching
things. Keep it a good place to be.

## Do

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to this.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Criticize ideas and code, never people.

## Don't

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, for any reason.
- Post sexual content, slurs, or personal information about others.
- Share links to pirated content, or ask others for them.
- Keep arguing after a maintainer has asked you to stop.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainers by opening a
[private report](https://github.com/abdvlrqhman/kinora/security/advisories/new),
or by writing to the account that owns the repository. Reports are kept
confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block anyone who
breaks these rules, in this repository and in every space connected to it.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
